How it works

  1. 1

    Read the home

    Smart meter and thermostat send hourly readings: tariff period, indoor and outdoor temperature, HVAC mode, setpoints and kWh.

  2. 2

    Check the rules

    Each reading is tested against the rules you have switched on. Turn a rule off and its alerts disappear.

  3. 3

    Suggest one action

    You get a short message plus a tip that explains why the change helps.

  4. 4

    You decide

    Accept to reschedule or adjust, or dismiss it. Your choices are remembered on this device.

About the project

Energy Advisor

Energy Advisor is a prototype energy-saving alert center for smart homes. It turns hourly IoT readings into short, practical suggestions so a household can cut cost without giving up comfort.

The data

The alerts shown here are sample rows from sic_IoT_test_dataset_251231.csv. Each row carries a timestamp, tariff period, HVAC mode, indoor and outdoor temperature (°C), cooling and heating setpoints, and energy use in kWh.

The rules

    Privacy

    Everything runs in your browser. Rule settings and your accept or dismiss choices are stored only on this device.
